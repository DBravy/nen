The headline
In the predictive lens, the coherent directions are keyed on the read state (the prev_token at t‑1), not the target token — exactly the intuition you had ("driven by what goes in"). And what they encode is almost always a construction (read-state word → its grammatical complement), not a standalone concept. That's why you see fewer "concept" patterns here than in the J‑lens: the J‑lens gives you token-identity categories; the predictive lens gives you "the model just emitted word X and is now composing the continuation of X." Once I aggregated on prev_token instead of the center token, clean families popped out immediately.

The finding I'd actually flag as important
A behavioral family is not necessarily one direction. I pulled the actual V columns and measured abs-cosine across layers:

Some families are a single residual direction tracked across a band of layers — SV37 need→to is 0.96–0.99 across L11–L13; ?→reconsider is one SV42 direction across L10–L13; the post‑<|message|> direction is 0.85–0.95 across L11–L15. These are true "tracked concepts" like your L13_SV49 "wait."
But the modal→be and So→conclusion families are behaviorally identical yet near-orthogonal across layers (cosine < 0.2). Same computation, re-implemented in a different direction at each layer.
So before calling a cross-layer pattern "the same concept," it's worth a 2-line cosine check — otherwise you'll conflate a genuinely persistent direction with a functional family that just happens to do the same job. The diagonal drift in SV rank (SV58→57→56…) that looks like tracking is real for group 1 and illusory for group 2.

The one-line summary
Low-effort CoT is "compute-then-close": numbers own the entire SV stack, plus a fixed scaffold (We need to…) and a sharp Provide-the-answer trigger. Medium-effort CoT repurposes the same directions toward epistemic exploration: the early/mid stack shifts off arithmetic onto hedged hypothesis language (might/could/maybe be) and contrastive pivots, and the "done, answer now" moment stops being a discrete detectable event. The directions the doc previously noted as "completely different in medium vs low" (L02_SV17, L13_SV54) are exactly this — directions whose top-slot ownership flips with effort.

One caveat to keep honest: medium sampled ~5× more tokens per layer (31,901 vs 6,572), so some of the number-family shrinkage is narrow content getting outcompeted for the top-64 slots. But the modal/contrastive/Provide/need shifts are corroborated by the raw base rates, so those are real behavioral differences, not sampling.
