# Blind word-judging task: REFERENCE, STANCE, FUNCTION or FRAGMENT

Below is an alphabetical list of English words (plus a few abbreviations and stray non-English items).
Label each word by its most usual role in ordinary web text. Answer one line per word, tab-separated:
word<TAB>LABEL, where LABEL is exactly one of:

- R  (REFERENCE) - the word mainly says WHAT is being talked about: nouns for things, materials,
     creatures, people, roles, places, organisations, products, events, activities, fields, body
     parts, times/dates/units; verbs of concrete or procedural action (walk, cook, send, build,
     transport); adjectives that classify or physically describe the referent (legal, clinical,
     residential, mechanical, wireless, red, large, national, online).
- S  (STANCE) - the word mainly expresses HOW the speaker stands toward what is said: modality and
     permission/obligation (may, might, must, should, shall, ought, need); certainty, doubt, hedging,
     approximation and focus (perhaps, probably, allegedly, potential, apparently, seems, just, only,
     simply, roughly, approximately, actually, really, exactly); intensity and degree (very, so,
     extremely, absolutely, huge, tiny, slightly); praise, blame and other evaluation (good, lovely,
     fantastic, awful, significant, important, useful, unique, solid, relevant); attitude, belief and
     desire verbs (think, believe, hope, trust, feel, want, love, hate); speech acts and assertion
     (say, claim, argue, suggest, promise, boast, agree); interjections and phatic talk (yeah, oh, lol,
     huh, thanks, please, welcome).
- F  (FUNCTION) - grammatical words: articles, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, auxiliaries and
     their contractions (the, of, and, is, was, to, can't, you're), quantifiers and numerals (all,
     some, few, more, two), linking adverbials (also, however, therefore, then), negation.
- X  (FRAGMENT/OTHER) - not a usable English word: sub-word pieces, prefixes, URLs, placeholders,
     non-English words, garbage.

Judge the word in isolation by its dominant everyday sense. Do not explain.
