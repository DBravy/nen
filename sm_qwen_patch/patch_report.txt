Reading guide. The strong claim (variance is conditional signal) predicts, for
topG and gnotj vs topJbar and pairL: larger g0, corr_pred near 1, high within_acc
with LOW cross_acc and LOW template stability, and g0^2/fSf of the same order.
Nonlinear early death (small alpha*) for G-sets with topJbar persisting would
instead say the variance was a small-signal artifact. High flip_frac says the
conditionality lives in discrete routing, below the Jacobian's resolution.
